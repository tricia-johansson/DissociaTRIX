About

My name is Trix (any pronouns - she/they/he), and I’m from Sweden. I live a slow life as an artist in Sweden’s second biggest city, Gothenburg. I moved the majority of my life in a rural little village, though and still adjusting to the big city life.

My artistic fields are digital art, traditional art, web coding, graphic design, animation, filmmaking, music composition, and photography. Yes, I have too many creative outlets for my own good, haha!

I live with DID and am part of the Tee-Jay system, C-PTSD, and several physical disabilities, and I like to educate people online.
